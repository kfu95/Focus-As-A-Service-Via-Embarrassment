Jail

Break a browsing rule and your tabs go to jail until Messages accepts your group alert.

Mac helper required

Jail requires a small Mac helper. It stays installed and is used only for the texting workflow: sending your alerts and importing contacts you choose. Jail cannot install software itself.

All chosen contacts will share one group chat. Each gets a message in that group: “Hi <first name>, I miss you”. Messages may reuse a group with exactly the same people.

Declining installation or a permission prompt after a violation keeps browsing locked. Install helper and review permissions.

    Contacts

    Phone numbers must use E.164 format, such as +14155552671. Changes are saved with settings.

    Mac Contacts access is optional and requested only when you click below. Allow Jail Helper in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Contacts.
